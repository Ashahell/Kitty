# Kitty: Core Awakening — Art Bible v1 (2026-10-31)

*The visual rules every asset follows: models, rooms, UI, animation, cutscenes and movie clips.
Sources: GDD v2 (§2, §3.2, §3.4, §4, §4.1, §6, §7, §12) and engine design rev 5 (§4.8, §4.13,
§6.3, §6.6). Where this bible and the GDD disagree, the GDD wins and this file is corrected. Owner: the
human art director (GDD D5, to be named before M3). Values marked **proposed** need art-director
sign-off.*

---

## 1. Pillars

1. **The platform is the style.** 320×256, a palette per era, flat faces, ordered dither. The CPU
   baseline (lavapipe) sets the look. NVK adds resolution and frame rate, never different content
   (GDD §4).
2. **Readable at a glance.** Each room fits one fixed view, so the whole puzzle is visible at once,
   clones included. Silhouette first, colour second, detail last.
3. **The OS is the world.** Rooms are Screens, alerts are Guru boxes, settings are `ENV:`. Every
   visual metaphor comes from real AROS/Amiga concepts, drawn in our own hand.
4. **Eras evolve.** Each chapter can use only the colours its era had. The palette progression is
   the story beat (GDD §3.2).
5. **Original and swappable.** Every branded asset is a skin. Nothing is copied from another work.

## 3. Palette system

- All colour is **palette indices** in vertex colours. There is no free RGB.
- Textures are pre-quantized at build time.
- `kitty-pal` rejects off-palette art when the asset is built (eng §6.3, §4.13).

### 3.1 Shading rule

- One palette index per face.
- **Light comes from the top-left**, the Workbench icon convention:
  - top faces take the lightest tone;
  - left faces the middle tone;
  - right faces the darkest.
- Lighting is baked by choosing the index. No runtime light.
- Gouraud blending between indices produces off-palette colours, so it is not used. See §11 Q2.

### 3.2 Slot map (proposed; needs the engine change in §11 E1)

| Slots | Use |
|---|---|
| 0–23 | the chapter palette (era colours plus their shading ramps) |
| 24 | Guru red (alert border) |
| 25 | Guru black (alert field) |
| 26–30 | five clone-stripe colours, fixed for the whole game, so clone 1 is always the same colour |
| 31 | UI focus highlight |

Slots 24–31 are "the System's own colours" and the only colours allowed outside an era.

### 3.3 Chapter palettes

All hex values are **proposed** and are our own values in the era's spirit.

| Ch | Era | Core colours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickstart 1.3 | 4: blue `#0055AA`, white `#FFFFFF`, black `#000022`, orange `#FF8800` | Blue fields, white edges, black shadow, orange accents. Shade with those 4 only |
| 2 | Workbench 2.x | 4: grey `#AAAAAA`, black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, blue `#6688BB` | 3D bevels: white top-left edge, black bottom-right edge |
| 3 | MagicWB-style | 8: greys `#959595 #7B7B7B #AFAFAF`, black, white, blue `#3B67A2`, beige `#AA907C`, rose `#FFA997` | Industrial; richer ramps. Palette in the style only; no MagicWB icons copied |
| 4 | Modern AROS (Zune/Wanderer) | up to 24: 4 ramps of 5 (green, grey, warm light, sky) + 4 accents | "Soft gradients" are stepped ramps, dithered at the edges, never blended |
| 5 | All eras | each screen region uses one earlier chapter's palette | Needs the 24-slot chapter area: Ch1 + Ch2 + Ch3 = 16 colours, plus ramps |

The Ch5 row is only possible because of the slot-map change in §3.2.

## 4. Shape language and modelling

- **Low-poly, faceted, clean.**
  - Big planar faces with a clear top, left and right; no noise.
  - Bevels are geometry (one chamfer), not textures.
- **Grid.** One room cell is one world unit. Props sit on the grid, gameplay objects fill whole
  cells, and nothing important straddles a cell edge.
- **Minimum readable size:** every gameplay object is at least 8 px in both dimensions at
  320×256, and identifiable as a one-colour silhouette.
- **Textures** (`P_tex`):
  - optional, 8×8 to 32×32, nearest-filtered, pre-quantized;
  - at most 4 textures per room (proposed);
  - used for signage, disk labels and screens, never for surface noise.
- **Two-sided geometry** only for thin props such as banners and leaves (`P_flat_nocull`).

### 4.1 Chapter environments

| Ch | Motif | Shape language | Materials (as palette tones) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Ancestor: one Core, many tasks | blocky, orthogonal, chunky platforms that tick in turn | flat enamel, blue and white |
| 2 | The Mirror: two living worlds | mirrored layouts, bevelled panels, window frames | brushed grey plates, blue glass |
| 3 | The Foundry | heavy machines, pipes, gantries, coolant channels; scale ×2 | grey metal, warm beige rust, rose heat glow |
| 4 | The Garden: low power | sparse, rounded by faceting, open sky, small light pools | greens, soft greys, warm lamps |
| 5 | The SMP Heart | regions in each era's palette, meeting at seams | every chapter's palette, each in its own region |
| Hub | Workbench neighbourhood | small houses for the modules, arcade cabinets, Shell kiosk | the current era; it fills up as chapters complete |

## 5. Characters

- **Kitty (licensed skin) and Process 1 (placeholder generic cat) share one rig.** Same
  proportions, silhouette, joint layout and animations. Every animation is authored once and
  plays on both skins. The game must look finished with Process 1 (GDD §2).
- **Kitty proportions** (proposed):
  - about 1 cell long and 0.75 cell tall; the head is a third of the height;
  - big ears and a long tail carry the expression;
  - the face is 3–5 polys with no texture needed.
- **The scarf** is the identity carrier:
  - it holds the clone stripe (palette slots 26–30);
  - it shows the scarf stripes earned from collected disks.
- **Clones** use the same mesh drawn with `P_dither` plus their stripe colour. A clone must never
  be confusable with Kitty: the dither and the stripe always show.
- **Modules** (Exec, Intuition, Graphics, DOS, Layers) are characters of architectural scale,
  each read through one prop:
  - Exec: a clock tower;
  - Intuition: windows / an eye motif with no eyes;
  - Graphics: a painter's easel and blitter;
  - DOS: shelves of disks;
  - Layers: overlapping panes.
  - Each one is frozen (dithered, stopped) until freed.
- **Nybbles:** tiny, about 0.4 cell, always in pairs (two make a byte), simple 4-pose loops.
- **Deadlock:** a shape, not a monster. A closed ring of links, one per held resource, that
  tightens. No teeth, no face beyond the cycle itself.
- **The Archivist:** visualised through `.readme` panels, not as a face.
- **The User:** never shown. Only the sky flickers.
- **Fred Fish:** never depicted (GDD D2). The disks, the credits dedication and the empty chair on
  the dock are enough.

## 6. Animation

- Keyframed vertex animation, blended two poses at a time by `VS_iso`; the weight comes from the
  tick in integer arithmetic.
- The tail and scarf are **authored keys, not physics**, so they look identical on every run
  (eng §4.8).
- **Timing is in 50 Hz ticks.** One cell of walking takes 8 ticks (`TICKS_PER_CELL = 8`).
  Anticipation takes 2–4 ticks, and squash-and-stretch goes up to ±20 % (proposed).
- **Snappy over smooth.** Hold poses, then move. At 25 fps the strong poses carry the motion.
- **Ch1 time-slicing must be visible.** Tasks move in turn, one at a time.
- **Frozen things stop dead** and switch to the dither pattern. Never slow-motion.

## 7. UI and screens

- **Every room is a Screen**, with a title bar showing the Screen name and a depth gadget in the
  corner that returns to the hub (GDD §4).
- **Gadget style follows the era:**
  - Ch1: flat;
  - Ch2: bevelled;
  - Ch3: MagicWB-style;
  - Ch4: Zune-style;
  - hub: the current era.
- **Font:** an original 8×8 bitmap font in the spirit of the Amiga system font. Topaz glyphs are
  not copied.
- **Text:** locale catalogs only (EN and DE in 1.0). Design for 1.5× the English length.
- **Guru / Cat Meditation box:**
  - slot 24 red border on a slot 25 black field, with the border blinking at 1 Hz (50 ticks per
    state);
  - text in the system font, e.g. "Software Failure. Press left mouse button to continue.";
  - used for deaths, Guru Alerts and validation errors.
- **HUD:** at most one line plus verb icons. Everything goes through `P_ui`.

## 8. Cutscenes and movie clips (engine requirement)

The engine must play both:

| Type | Use for | How |
|---|---|---|
| **In-engine cutscene** (default) | story beats, module freed, chapter intros, the finale | A scripted sequence played through the replay/input-log path and the normal renderer. Same rooms, palette and pipelines. Deterministic, the same on both tiers, re-skins automatically, almost no disk cost |
| **Movie clip** | what rooms can't show: boot intro, warm-reboot flash, credits | Pre-rendered frames decoded on the CPU and shown as one full-screen `P_tex`/`P_ui` quad. No new shader |

### 8.1 Rules for both

- Skippable with fire or Esc. Never the only place where gameplay information appears.
- Subtitles come from the locale catalogs and are drawn in the system font.
- Same canvas as gameplay: 320×256, 25 fps, letterbox only by framing (no bars beyond the
  Screen).

### 8.2 Movie-clip format (proposed)

- Palette-indexed, using the current chapter palette plus the system slots.
- Delta-compressed frames, Amiga ANIM-style (only changed spans per frame).
- Sound is a MOD or samples, synced by tick.
- Limits: each clip ≤ 60 s; all clips together ≤ 32 MB in 1.0.
- Decoding must stay inside the 40 ms baseline frame time on lavapipe.

### 8.3 Skins

- In-engine cutscenes re-skin for free.
- A movie clip may show Kitty or AROS branding only if a Process 1 variant of the same clip
  ships too. Otherwise the clip shows no branded character, or the beat is done in-engine.

## 9. Licensing guard-rails (from GDD §2)

- No ReBoot elements at all.
- No other work's names, catchphrases, logos or coined terms.
- Homage is through genre and mechanics only.
- The Boing! disk uses our own checkered sphere model. Disk numbers start at FF #1121.
- No falling-tetromino imagery.
- Every branded asset lives in a skin package. Assets stay gated until the skin licence clears.
- Post-launch community art needs a licence field: CC-BY-4.0, CC-BY-SA-4.0 or GPL-3.0-or-later.

## 10. Pipeline and checks

- **Build-time checks:**
  - `kitty-pal` rejects off-palette vertex colours and textures;
  - the validator checks the §2.1 triangle budgets and the texture count per asset and per room.
- **Model sources:** one folder per asset, with the mesh, its palette-index map and its keys.
  Skin assets go under `skins/<skin>/`, with Process 1 as the default.
- **Review gate for every asset:**
  - silhouette readable at 1× on 320×256;
  - correct era palette;
  - top-left light;
  - within budget;
  - Process 1 variant present.
- **Room review:** a 1× screenshot of the full room next to the solver's PAR path. The puzzle must
  be readable without moving the camera.
