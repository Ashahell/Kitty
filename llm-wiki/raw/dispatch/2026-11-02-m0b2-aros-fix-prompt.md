## What this task is

M0b.1 (the M0b fix round) ran no guest command. Its runner's spike server died at import, so the guest
agent's hello went to a leftover server that did not read the runner's spool; three boots ran on a
grant of 2 while S14 was FAIL, and `build/m0b1/boots.txt` was cut back by hand to one line (review:
`~/Work/Kitty/Docs/Development/m0b1_aros_critique.md`). M0b.2 fixes what that review found and
nothing else:

  before it writes the ledger or boots, and refuses when ports 9099/9100 are taken.
- **Boot ledger** (D41): append-only, cross-checked against the lanes' own boot records.
- **S2.interfaces** (D42): an owner decision; out of scope here.

`build/m0b2` and the prompt path as this file), the Linux gates, frames 0/150/299 byte-identical to
